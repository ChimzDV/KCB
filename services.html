Floor Plans & Elevations

Detailed dimensioned room layouts, sectional views, and 4-sided elevation illustrations showing window positions, wall heights, and finishes.

  • Precise internal dimensions
  • Cross-sectional slice views
  • Front, rear, & side elevations

Foundation & Roof Layout

Engineered strip or raft foundation layouts, column placement details, roof pitch calculations, truss designs, and rainwater drainage paths.

  • Foundation footing depths
  • Truss & rafter schedules
  • Roof drainage slopes

Plumbing & Drainage

Schematic diagrams indicating water supply piping, waste drainage systems, soakaway sizing, and septic tank layouts.

  • Water supply & pipe runs
  • Plumbing run schematics
  • Septic & soakaway sizing

Door & Window Schedules

Accurate schedules listing exact sizes, materials, opening orientations, and quantities for seamless procurement with suppliers and carpenters.

  • Standardized opening sizes
  • Glass & frame specifications
  • Hardware & lintel details
