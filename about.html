About Our Company

King Class Building Ltd.

King Class Building Ltd. is an architectural drafting and building planning firm dedicated to creating modern, durable, and cost-effective residential and commercial blueprints.

We believe that every successful construction project starts with a thoughtfully planned design. Our mission is to bridge the gap between architectural aspiration and practical building execution, delivering drawing sets that contractors can build from with total confidence.

  • Complete, permit-ready architectural and structural drawing sets
  • Customizable 2, 3, 4 bedroom and multi-floor plan configurations
  • Professional architectural verification and technical site assessments

From initial concept ideation to plan modifications and quantity estimations, our team works closely with each client to ensure every square meter serves their lifestyle and budget.
