Complete Construction Blueprint Package

Each house plan produced by King Class Building Ltd. is drafted as a comprehensive, permit-ready construction document set. Whether you are building in an urban subdivision or on an independent rural plot, our technical drawings provide full clarity for structural engineers, council approval inspectors, and site contractors.

What Is Included in This Plan Set:

Dimensioned Floor Plans

Exact wall placements, room square meterages, door swings, and window locations.

4-Sided Elevations

Front, back, left, and right architectural views with finished floor-to-ceiling heights.

Foundation & Section Layouts

Trench depths, concrete footing details, column schedules, and slab reinforcement.

Roof Framing & Drainage

Truss spacing, rafter dimensions, pitch angles, gutter runs, and downpipe locations.

Electrical & Plumbing Schematics

Switches, lights, power sockets, water supply lines, waste drainage, and septic tank layout.

Door & Window Schedules

Detailed sizing specifications for timber/aluminium joinery procurement.

Need custom adjustments to this plan? We can easily expand bedrooms, adjust room layouts, change the roof style, or mirror the plan to match your plot orientation.

Plan Specifications

  • Plan Category Residential
  • Bedrooms 2, 3, or 4 Beds
  • Bathrooms 1 to 3.5 Baths
  • Minimum Plot Size 15m x 20m (or larger)
  • Roofing Style Modern Pitch / Hip
