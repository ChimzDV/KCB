Comprehensive Architectural Verification & Plan Validation

Before investing significant capital in building materials and contractor labour, having your building plans professionally verified is essential. King Class Building Ltd. provides meticulous technical reviews to identify dimensional errors, structural weaknesses, ventilation oversights, and council non-compliance issues before they result in expensive on-site alterations.

Key Steps in Our Verification Process:

  • Dimensional & Grid Audit: Verifying that all internal room measurements, wall thicknesses, and hallway widths match standard living clearances.
  • Load Path & Structural Feasibility: Checking column positions, beam spans, and load-bearing walls against expected roof and slab loads.
  • Regulatory & Setback Compliance: Confirming boundary setbacks, natural lighting percentages, and ceiling heights comply with local building regulations.
  • Service Routing Coordination: Ensuring plumbing runs and septic drainage lines do not conflict with foundation or structural elements.

Upon completing our verification audit, we provide you with a structured feedback report highlighting recommended corrections, optional enhancements, and a certified set of drawings ready for permit submission and construction execution.
