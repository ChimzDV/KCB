How to Choose the Right Bedroom Configuration for Your Plot

KCB Architectural Team House Plans & Design

Selecting the ideal house plan is one of the most critical decisions in any building journey. Before purchasing a blueprint or commencing foundation work, evaluating how your plot size, lifestyle demands, and budget align with different bedroom configurations will ensure long-term satisfaction.

1. 2-Bedroom Plans (2BHK) — Efficient & Budget-Friendly

2BHK plans are ideal for starter homes, retirement living, or rental investment properties on smaller plots (e.g. 15m x 20m). They maximize internal living space while keeping roof truss spans and foundation costs within an accessible budget.

2. 3-Bedroom Plans (3BHK) — The Universal Family Standard

3BHK designs offer a great balance for growing families. They typically feature a master bedroom with an en-suite bathroom, two secondary bedrooms, a shared bathroom, open-plan dining, and a dedicated kitchen pantry.

3. 4-Bedroom & Multi-Floor Plans — Space & Flexibility

For larger families, executive builds, or plots where vertical expansion is preferred, 4-bedroom and multi-floor configurations allow for home offices, guest accommodations, and private upper-floor sleeping quarters.
